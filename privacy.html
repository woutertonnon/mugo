Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

This policy covers the Mugo app for iOS, including its beta versions distributed through TestFlight, and this website.

In short: Mugo has no accounts and no tracking, analytics or advertising. The developer does not collect or receive any of your personal data through the app. Your location stays on your device.

Who is responsible

Mugo is developed by an individual developer based in Switzerland. For any question about this policy or your data, write to hello@mugo.ch.

What the app does not collect

  • No account, sign-in, name or email address.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising software inside the app, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • The app sends no data to the developer or to any server the developer runs.

Your location

The app asks for permission to use your location only while the app is in use, and only after you tap the locate button. The position is used to move the map to where you are and to draw your position on it. It is processed on your device only. The app does not store it, send it, or share it with anyone. You can withdraw the permission at any time under Settings → Mugo → Location. The rest of the app works without it.

The background map

The camping rules and the chance map are inside the app. The background map is not: its images ("tiles") are downloaded from the Swiss Federal Office of Topography (swisstopo, wmts.geo.admin.ch) as you move around the map. As with any web request, swisstopo's servers receive your IP address and the tiles requested, which shows roughly which area you are looking at. The app does not send your GPS position with these requests. swisstopo handles this data under its own terms and privacy statement.

Links to sources

When you tap a link to a law, an ordinance or another source, it opens in your web browser, outside the app. The site you visit then applies its own privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a few settings on your device, such as the language you prefer for quotes and where the map was last shown. They never leave the device and are removed when you delete the app.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test a beta version, Apple's TestFlight service may share some information with the developer: the name and email address you were invited with, your device model and iOS version, usage and crash data, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. Apple processes this under its own privacy policy and the TestFlight terms. The developer uses it only to run the beta and fix problems, and does not pass it on to anyone else. If you email the developer, your message and address are used only to answer you.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and the GDPR where it applies, you may ask what personal data is held about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Since the app collects none, this will usually only concern emails or TestFlight feedback you have sent. Write to the address above.

Changes

If the app's handling of data changes, for example if a purchase or a new feature is added, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
