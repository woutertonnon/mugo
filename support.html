Testing the beta

  2. Open the invitation link on your iPhone, or ask for one by email.
  3. Install TentWise from TestFlight. Builds expire after 90 days and TestFlight offers the update.

Feedback: take a screenshot while using the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight → TentWise → Send Beta Feedback. Tell us the spot (a screenshot of it is enough), what the app said, and what you think it should say.

Frequently asked

Does a high percentage mean camping is allowed?

No. Switzerland has no general right to camp in the wild. The percentage estimates how likely a single, considerate night in a tent is to be tolerated if you have asked nobody. A landowner, a sign or a local authority always has the last word. Leave no trace.

Why is a spot low when the commune never said anything?

The number is the lowest of the federal, cantonal and communal chances. A federal protected area, a cantonal nature reserve, forest, a nearby building or private farmland can each lower it on their own. Tap the spot to see which rule sets the number.

I know a rule the app is missing or has wrong.

Please email it, if possible with a link to the ordinance, the sign or the commune's own page. Every rule in the app is quoted from a source, so a source is what lets us correct it.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. The rules and the chance map are inside the app. The background map needs a connection, so offline you see the chance layer on a blank background.

The locate button does nothing.

Check Settings → TentWise → Location is set to While Using the App. The app uses your location only to show where you are. See the privacy policy.
